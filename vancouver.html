Why build in Vancouver?

A Pacific-time base that overlaps the Americas, Europe and Asia, a deep engineering talent pool, strong IP and privacy law, and trade agreements that connect Canada to the world.

Six reasons clients hire here.

A time zone that overlaps

Pacific time overlaps the Americas during the working day, Europe in the morning and Asia in the evening, so check-ins happen live.

Trade agreements

Canada trades under agreements such as CUSMA with the US and Mexico, CETA with the EU and CPTPP across the Pacific. They support cross-border services and digital trade. Rules change, so confirm your situation with a trade advisor.

Cost advantage

Engineering rates are competitive with other major tech hubs, and we quote in CAD, with other currencies on request.

Talent and universities

UBC, SFU and BCIT feed one of North America's larger tech talent pools, with a growing AI and clean-tech community.

Privacy and IP law

Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and common-law contracts. Written IP assignment keeps ownership with you.

Easy to reach

Vancouver is a major international airport hub, so in-person workshops are simple to schedule when you want them.
