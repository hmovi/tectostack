Why build in Vancouver?

Same time zone as Seattle and San Francisco, a deep engineering talent pool, strong IP and privacy law, and a trade relationship with the US built on CUSMA.

Six reasons US companies hire here.

Same time zone

Pacific time, like Seattle, Portland and San Francisco. Daily standups happen live, not overnight.

CUSMA trade access

Canada, the US and Mexico trade under CUSMA, the successor to NAFTA. It includes a digital trade chapter that covers cross-border services and electronic transmission. Tariff and trade rules change, so confirm your situation with a trade advisor.

Cost advantage

Engineering rates are often lower than Seattle and Bay Area equivalents, and invoicing in CAD can help US budgets.

Talent and universities

UBC, SFU and BCIT feed one of North America's larger tech talent pools, with a growing AI and clean-tech community.

Privacy and IP law

Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and common-law contracts. Written IP assignment keeps ownership with you.

Close and reachable

Seattle is a short drive or flight away, so in-person workshops are easy to schedule.
